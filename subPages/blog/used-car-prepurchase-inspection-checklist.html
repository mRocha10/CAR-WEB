Used Car Pre-Purchase Inspection Checklist

A used car can look clean and still hide expensive issues. This checklist keeps the first inspection focused on the places where money and risk usually show up first.

Exterior and tyres

  • Check for uneven panel gaps, paint mismatch, and tyre wear differences.
  • Look for wheel damage or tyres worn badly on one side.

Engine and fluids

  • Inspect leaks, warning lights, cold-start behaviour, and idle quality.
  • Ask for service history, especially timing system and oil changes.

Road test

  • Listen for suspension knocks, transmission hesitation, and steering pull.
  • Test braking feel and check whether the car tracks straight.

Once a used vehicle passes the initial checks, compare similar alternatives in the comparison tool to avoid overpaying for the first acceptable option.
