How to Choose Brake Pads: Ceramic vs Semi-Metallic

Ceramic pads usually suit drivers who want quieter braking, less visible brake dust, and predictable daily use. Semi-metallic pads tend to suit heavier braking cycles, performance driving, or drivers who accept more noise and dust in exchange for stronger bite.

Ceramic pads

These often run quieter and cleaner in normal commuting conditions. They can cost more up front, but they are attractive to drivers who care about refinement and lower visible wheel dust.

Semi-metallic pads

These usually handle heat better and can deliver more aggressive braking feel. The trade-off is that they can create more dust, more noise, and harsher low-speed feel.

How to decide

  • Choose ceramic if your car is mostly used for commuting and daily errands.
  • Choose semi-metallic if you regularly brake hard, tow, or drive in more demanding conditions.
  • Always match pad choice to rotor condition and manufacturer fitment requirements.
