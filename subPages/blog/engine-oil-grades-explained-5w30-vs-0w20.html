Engine Oil Grades Explained: 5W-30 vs 0W-20

Oil grade affects cold-start behaviour, fuel economy, and protection under load. The right answer is usually the manufacturer specification, not whichever oil sounds â€œstronger.â€

0W-20

This grade is commonly used where manufacturers prioritise fuel efficiency and good cold-start flow. It is often found in newer engines designed around thinner oils.

5W-30

This grade can offer a slightly thicker operating viscosity and is common in engines designed for it, especially where broader operating conditions or older designs are involved.

Decision rule

  • Follow the manufacturer specification first.
  • Use climate and driving conditions as a secondary factor, not the primary one.
  • Choose quality oil and service intervals consistently rather than changing viscosity casually.
