Brand History

Founded in 1950, SEAT (Sociedad Espanola de Automoviles de Turismo) represents Spanish automotive passion and innovation. As part of the Volkswagen Group, SEAT combines German engineering precision with Mediterranean design flair to create dynamic, sporty vehicles for the modern driver.

Throughout its history, SEAT has evolved from producing licensed Fiat models to developing its own distinctive vehicles, becoming Spain's largest automotive manufacturer and a significant player in the European market.

Current Brand Focus

  • Urban Mobility: Smart solutions for city transportation
  • Sporty Character: Dynamic driving experience across the range
  • Electrification: Developing sustainable mobility solutions
  • Design Innovation: Contemporary Spanish aesthetics
