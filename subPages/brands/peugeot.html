What matters most about Peugeot

Peugeot generally appeals to buyers who want distinctive European design and efficient everyday packaging without moving to a premium badge.

Context still matters because the brand's history usually explains why buyers still read it the way they do today. Founded in 1810, Peugeot began as a steel foundry before evolving into one of the world's oldest automotive manufacturers. With a legacy spanning over two centuries, Peugeot combines French elegance with cutting-edge technology to create distinctive vehicles that embody their slogan 'Motion & Emotion'.
