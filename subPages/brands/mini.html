Context still matters: Born in 1959, MINI revolutionized small car design with its innovative transverse engine layout. Today, under BMW ownership, MINI continues to blend its iconic British heritage with modern technology and premium quality.

Background and lineup

Mini history, current focus, and model context

Use the supporting background below to understand how Mini built its reputation and where the current lineup fits after you have already framed the buyer decision.

Brand History

Born in 1959, MINI revolutionized small car design with its innovative transverse engine layout. Today, under BMW ownership, MINI continues to blend its iconic British heritage with modern technology and premium quality.

MINI's journey has been marked by innovation, sustainability, and a commitment to providing exceptional driving experiences. The company has expanded its reach to various markets, including Europe, Asia, and beyond.

Current Brand Focus

  • Design Heritage: Maintaining iconic MINI styling cues
  • Urban Mobility: Creating perfect city-friendly vehicles
  • Driving Fun: Delivering go-kart handling experience
  • Electrification: Developing sustainable urban transport solutions
