What matters most about Aston Martin

Aston Martin is primarily a grand-touring luxury-performance brand, best suited to buyers chasing exclusivity, design, and occasion value.

Context still matters because the brand's history usually explains why buyers still read it the way they do today. Founded in 1913, Aston Martin has become synonymous with luxury, performance, and timeless British style. From its iconic association with James Bond to its racing heritage at Le Mans, Aston Martin continues to create some of the world's most desirable sports cars and grand tourers.

Shortlist advice

When Aston Martin is a smart shortlist, and when it needs more caution

Let the badge narrow the field, but let daily use, running cost, and the right model finish the decision.

Shortlist Aston Martin if...

  • Buyers prioritising exclusivity, theatre, and high-end design over everyday practicality.
  • Drivers who want GT comfort with strong performance rather than track-first extremity.
  • Shoppers comparing Ferrari, Bentley, McLaren, or top-tier Mercedes-AMG products.

Pause before committing if...

Background and lineup

Aston Martin history, current focus, and model context

Brand History

Founded in 1913, Aston Martin has become synonymous with luxury, performance, and timeless British style. From its iconic association with James Bond to its racing heritage at Le Mans, Aston Martin continues to create some of the world's most desirable sports cars and grand tourers.

Throughout its history, Aston Martin has maintained an unwavering commitment to craftsmanship and excellence. The company's racing success, particularly at prestigious events like the 24 Hours of Le Mans, has influenced its approach to creating exceptional road cars.

Current Brand Focus

Today, Aston Martin continues to evolve while maintaining its core values. The brand's focus areas include:

  • Performance Excellence: Developing cutting-edge sports cars
  • Luxury Craftsmanship: Maintaining exceptional build quality
  • Racing Heritage: Continuing success in motorsport
  • Future Innovation: Exploring electrification and sustainable performance
