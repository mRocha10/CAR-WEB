Buyer-focused summary

Sports cars are best for buyers who actively prioritise driving involvement and are comfortable accepting clear comfort and practicality compromises.

Who this body style suits best

  • Enthusiasts buying primarily for handling and driver engagement.
  • Second-car buyers or those with limited passenger needs.
  • Drivers choosing emotion and road feel over versatility.

Common trade-offs to watch

  • Ride comfort, luggage space, and daily usability usually take a hit.
  • Insurance and tyre costs can be much higher than ordinary coupes.
  • Some buyers really want a fast GT or hot hatch, not a true sports car.

What to compare next

  • Driver engagement and steering feel.
  • Ride quality on roads you actually use.
  • Insurance, tyres, and storage practicality.
