Editorial Policy

How Engine Starters approaches accuracy, corrections, and commercial separation.

The site is built to explain trade-offs, connect technical detail to real ownership decisions, and keep advertising separate from editorial judgement.

  • Explain trade-offsnot one-spec answers that hide the rest of the ownership picture.
  • Correct mistakeswhen readers identify factual issues worth fixing.
  • Separate adsfrom the conclusions and recommendations inside the guides.

Automotive advice is only useful when it connects the technical details to the checks and compromises a buyer can actually understand.

Content principles

  • Explain trade-offs and limitations instead of treating one specification as the whole decision.
  • Distinguish examples from currently available models; confirm prices and specifications for the market, year, and trim you are considering.
  • Correct factual mistakes when readers identify them.

Commercial independence

Advertising may appear on the site. Advertisers do not determine the recommendations or conclusions in the guides.

How to report a correction

If you identify an error, use the contact page with the affected URL, the proposed correction, and any supporting source that helps verify the change.

The faster the note points to the exact problem, the faster it can be reviewed.
