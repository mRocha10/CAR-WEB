Editorial Policy

Engine Starters aims to publish practical, structured automotive content that helps readers understand vehicle categories, compare options, and make better buying decisions.

Content principles

  • Prioritise clarity over hype.
  • Match each page to a clear search and reader intent.
  • Avoid thin or misleading page titles.
  • Correct factual issues when they are identified.

Commercial independence

Advertising may appear on the site, but editorial structure and published guidance are designed around usefulness first.

Corrections

If you identify an error, please use the contact page with the affected URL and the proposed correction.
