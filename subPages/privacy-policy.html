Privacy Policy

Engine Starters is a public information site. This page explains the requests your browser makes and the information you may choose to send us.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process routine requests, including IP address, browser information and requested pages, to deliver the site and protect its service. Pages also request fonts from Google Fonts and some images from a third-party image service; those providers may receive your IP address and request metadata.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense is loaded on some pages. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising delivery and measurement, depending on your region and their settings. Read Google's advertising information for details. The site currently does not offer its own cookie-preference panel; browser settings may let you restrict cookies. This description is not a substitute for any legally required consent mechanism.

Comparison links

Vehicle selections in the comparison tool can appear in the page URL. Avoid sharing a comparison link if you do not want others to see your selected vehicles.

Contact email

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email there. The details you send, including your address and message, are used to review and reply to your enquiry.

Changes

This notice may change as the site's services change. Check this page for the current description of how the site works.
