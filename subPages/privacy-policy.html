Privacy Policy

How Engine Starters handles site requests, third-party services, and direct contact.

This page explains the routine requests your browser makes, how advertising and external services may operate, and what happens when you choose to contact the site directly.

  • Hostingroutine requests are processed to deliver pages and keep the service running.
  • AdsGoogle AdSense may appear on some pages depending on the section and region.
  • Contactemail drafts are only sent when you choose to send them from your own mail app.

As with the technology inside a car, it helps to know which services a site relies on and what those services can see.

Hosting and external resources

The hosting provider may process routine requests, including IP address, browser information, and requested pages, to deliver the site and protect its service. Pages also request fonts from Google Fonts; that provider may receive your IP address and request metadata. Site illustrations are served from this site's own image directory.

Advertising and cookies

Google AdSense is loaded on some pages. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for advertising delivery and measurement, depending on your region and their settings. Read Google's advertising information for details. The site currently does not offer its own cookie-preference panel; browser settings may let you restrict cookies. This description is not a substitute for any legally required consent mechanism.

Comparison links

Vehicle selections in the comparison tool can appear in the page URL. Avoid sharing a comparison link if you do not want others to see your selected vehicles.

Contact email

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email there. The details you send, including your address and message, are used to review and reply to your enquiry.

Changes

This notice may change as the site's services change. Check this page for the current description of how the site works.
