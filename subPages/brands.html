Browse major car brands by market position, region, and likely buyer fit.

Start here if you already know the kind of car you want and need to judge which brands usually suit that job best.

Luxury-focused brands

Usually strongest on refinement, badge appeal, materials, and technology, but often more expensive to buy and maintain.

  • Mercedes-BenzPremium comfort and technology
  • BMWLuxury with a stronger dynamics reputation
  • LexusLuxury paired with reliability focus

Mainstream volume brands

Usually better starting points for buyers prioritising value, parts access, and everyday practicality.

  • ToyotaReliability and broad category coverage
  • HondaBalanced ownership value and engineering reputation
  • FordStrong truck, SUV, and performance presence

EV and innovation-heavy brands

Strong candidates when electric driving, battery strategy, or software-heavy ownership are central to the decision.

  • TeslaSoftware-led EV ownership profile
  • BYDBattery and EV scale story
  • NIOPremium EV positioning and battery swapping
