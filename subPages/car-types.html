Vehicle category research

Choose the right body style before you fall in love with a specific model.

Vehicle type often decides comfort, practicality, running cost, and day-to-day ease before brand even enters the conversation. Start here if you are still choosing the kind of car that suits your life best.

SUVs, minivans, and crossovers usually win on space, flexible seating, and everyday utility.

How to decide between types

  • Start with passengers and cargoSpace and seating needs usually remove the wrong options quickly.
  • Estimate annual driving costFuel economy and tyre size matter more over time than most shoppers expect.
  • Match the body style to your roadsUrban parking, rough weather, and highway travel all change what feels practical.

Where to narrow things next

If one body style already feels right, move into brand profiles or a direct vehicle comparison next. That is where you can test the category against real prices, cabins, MPG, and ownership trade-offs.
