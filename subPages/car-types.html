Vehicle category research

Choose the right body style before you fall in love with a specific model.

Vehicle type is often the biggest decision-maker for comfort, practicality, efficiency, insurance, and long-term ownership costs. This page is designed to help readers narrow the field before moving into brand and model-level comparisons.

Best for families

SUVs, minivans, and crossovers usually win on space, flexible seating, and everyday utility.

Best for efficiency

Compact cars, hatchbacks, and many sedans usually keep running costs lower and city driving simpler.

Best for lifestyle-specific needs

Pickups, sports cars, convertibles, and luxury vehicles serve more specialised priorities and trade-offs.

How to decide between types

  • Start with passengers and cargoSpace and seating needs usually remove the wrong options quickly.
  • Estimate annual driving costFuel economy and tyre size matter more over time than most readers expect.
  • Match the body style to your roadsUrban parking, rough weather, and highway travel all change what feels practical.
